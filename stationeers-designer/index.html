How to use

Grid: one tile = one large grid cell (2 m × 2 m × 2 m = 8 m³). Each tile holds one structure, one device, one cable, one pipe and one chute at the same time.

Tools: B brush · L line · F filled rect · O outline rect · M room (walls + floor) · E erase · I pick · V inspect. R rotates the device. Right/middle-drag or Space-drag pans, wheel zooms. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo.

Rooms are floor tiles enclosed by walls, windows or doors. A room touching empty space or an open frame is flagged as leaking. Devices dropped on bare ground get a floor automatically (except outdoor ones like solar panels).

Power: cables join to neighbouring cables. A device is powered by the cable on its own tile (or, failing that, a neighbouring one). Each network compares generation, demand and the weakest cable rating.

Gas: pipes join the same way; vents, pumps and tanks need a pipe. Liquid pipes are a separate class and never join gas pipes.

Levels: use the level tabs to design multiple decks. Analysis runs per level; stairs/ladders are markers only.
